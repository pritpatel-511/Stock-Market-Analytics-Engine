🏆 Project Name
Stock Market Analytics Engine
Professional Financial Data Analysis using Python & NumPy
🎯 Project Goal

Imagine you are hired as a Junior Quantitative Data Analyst.

Your company gives you one year of stock market data for multiple companies.

Your job is to generate complete reports that help investors understand:

Which stock performed best?
Which one is safest?
Which one is most volatile?
Which stock is suitable for long-term investment?
Which company has strong momentum?

You are not predicting prices.

You are analyzing historical data.

This is exactly what many data analysts do.
